Rules

Rank is what you pay. Nothing else.

01

Three boards. All-time is the total ever paid and never expires. Today resets at midnight IST. Daily is one board per day, archived. Each board can be viewed by city and by category.

02

New listings start at ₹199, in whole rupees.

03

Taking #1 costs at least ₹100 more than the current #1. Paying less still lists you at whatever rank that amount reaches. Equal amounts: the earlier listing ranks higher.

04

Already listed? Bid again on the same link. Checkout only charges the difference.

05

Indian D2C brands only. A brand website or Instagram handle you own. Valid business details (GST or company info) required.

06

Not allowed: adult content; betting or real-money gaming; alcohol, tobacco and vapes; drugs; weapons; crypto or investment schemes; MLM; medical cure claims; political or religious content; hate, caste or communal content; counterfeit goods; misleading claims; chat or invite links, URL shorteners and tracking parameters.

07

Removals. We can remove any listing that breaks these rules. Removed listings are refunded at our discretion.

08

Payments via UPI, cards and netbanking (Razorpay). A completed payment claims the rank. Receipt/invoice for every payment. Payments are otherwise non-refundable.

09

Paid listing, always labelled. Every listing shows a "Paid listing" tag. Ranks are bought, not earned.

10

Visibility only. Boli sells visibility only. We don't guarantee clicks, traffic, followers or sales.

11

Rivalry posts. We only tag brands that are listed and have opted in to rivalry posts. No mocking or disparaging any brand.

12

Founder consent. Founder photos and names appear only with the founder's consent, and can be removed on request.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Boli?

A public leaderboard where Indian D2C brands pay for a listing and rank by their bid. The highest bid holds the #1 spot.

Is this gambling or a game?

No. You pay a fixed, disclosed amount for a public listing. There's no chance, no prize and no winnings. It's paid visibility, like an ad slot.

Why would anyone pay?

Visibility. Every rank change is shared publicly, and the top spots are the first thing shoppers see. Boli sells visibility only. We don't guarantee clicks, traffic, followers or sales.

When does it launch?

Monday, 16 November 2026, 8 PM IST.

What does it cost?

From ₹199. #1 costs at least ₹100 more than the current #1.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes, a receipt/invoice for every payment.

Can I get a refund if I'm outbid?

No. Payments are non-refundable, and you can always raise your bid. The one exception: if we remove a listing for breaking the rules, refunds are at our discretion.

Is this like Product Hunt?

No votes, no hunters, no review queue. Just the bid.
