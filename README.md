# Boli: India's Public Leaderboard for D2C Brands

**Live:** https://boliboard.pages.dev  ·  Design: Ivory & Ink (calm ivory, ink type, gold reserved for #1)


**Bid boldly. Hold #1.**

Boli is a pay-to-rank public leaderboard where Indian D2C brands and startups pay for visibility. Every rupee and every click is shown openly. Inspired by outbid.lol, rebuilt for the Indian market.

> Status: pre-launch concept. Launch planned for 16 November 2026, 8:00 PM IST. All boards on the page use clearly labelled **sample data** with fictional brands.

## How it works
- **₹199** gets a brand onto the board.
- To take **#1**, bid at least **₹100 more** than the current #1 (whole rupees only).
- Boards: **All-time**, **Today** (resets at midnight IST), and archived **Daily** winners.
- **City boards** (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Jaipur) and **category boards** (Skincare & Beauty, Snacks & Food, Fashion, Health & Fitness, Home, Startups).

## Features
- Founder spotlight: the #1 card shows the founder's face and story (with consent)
- Shareable "Today's #1" and "Defending #1" badges
- A visitor discount code on every listing, plus a Deals board for shoppers
- A rivalry feed of overtakes
- A countdown, waitlist, bid-guess slider (just for fun, no money involved), and a claim modal
- Transparent click metrics, with no fake activity

## Trust and compliance (India)
- A bid buys a **paid listing**, nothing more. No chance, no prize, no winnings.
- Every listing is tagged **Paid listing**.
- A prohibited-content policy covering betting, alcohol and tobacco, crypto schemes, MLM, political and religious content, misleading claims, and more.
- No guarantees of traffic or sales. It sells visibility only.
- A privacy note on the waitlist, and a receipt for every payment.
- Payments are planned through Razorpay.

## Tech
- A single static page: HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript
- No build step. Open `index.html` to run it.
- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages (free tier)
